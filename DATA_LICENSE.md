# Data rights and attribution

The MIT license in this repository covers our original code and documentation.
It does **not** grant rights to Magic: The Gathering card names, rules text,
trademarks, artwork, third-party model weights, or third-party software.

Oracle targets come from [Scryfall bulk data](https://scryfall.com/docs/api/bulk-data).
Printed wording comes from [MTGJSON](https://mtgjson.com/), whose
[license page](https://mtgjson.com/license/) distinguishes its own work from
Wizards of the Coast material. Magic: The Gathering and its card content belong
to Wizards of the Coast and other respective rights holders. This project is
unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by those organizations.
No artwork is distributed. Descriptions include model-generated paraphrases of
card mechanics; publishing them does not place the underlying card content
under MIT or into the public domain. The complete dataset is therefore labeled
as mixed-rights data, without claiming a blanket open license over its contents.

The optional mtgish parser is downloaded separately at a pinned revision; its
MIT license is retained with the extracted source. Its grammar is unchanged.
Base model weights are downloaded separately under their model's license.
Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 uses Apache 2.0. Adapter releases must preserve base-model
attribution and identify the exact base revision and training dataset.
