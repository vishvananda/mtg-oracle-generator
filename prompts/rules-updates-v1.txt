Verified rules update, September 2025 (also confirmed by the 2026 Marvel Super
Heroes release notes): modal double-faced cards CAN transform. A modal
double-faced permanent transforms when instructed if the other face has a
permanent type. It can enter transformed if its back face has a permanent type.
Both faces being independently castable is compatible with an ability that
transforms between them. Do not reject such a reference card or description
merely because its layout is modal_dfc; do not change it to transform layout.
A spell face (instant or sorcery) cannot be turned into a permanent by transforming.
