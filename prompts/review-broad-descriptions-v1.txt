For cases marked description_policy="plausible_completion", use a different
criterion from exact paraphrase cases: could reference_draft reasonably be a
completed card for this description? It need not be the only valid completion.
Do not require reconstruction of the source card or coverage of every field.

Pass short general descriptions that convey a compatible gameplay idea while
leaving numbers, costs, starting loyalty, triggers, limitations, secondary
abilities, or face details unspecified. A generic creature concept is sufficient
for an otherwise simple creature. For a mechanically distinctive card, prefer a
recognizable gameplay idea over an empty "make a card" request. Do not reject
merely because an omitted detail appears in the proposed completion.

Reject concrete contradictions or concepts the card does not realize: mill
versus exile, reversed draw/discard order, wrong player, mutually incompatible
card types, a claimed free/unrestricted ability that actually has a cost or
restriction, or mismatched explicit numbers. Unspecified is not the same as
unrestricted. Use uncertain for genuinely unclear compatibility, not merely
for an omitted implementation detail. Reject exhaustive full-card restatements
for broad_plain/broad_shorthand; these styles should sound like general requests.
Cases without this policy still require exact fidelity to expressed constraints.
